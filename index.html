The Story

The Architecture
Born from the eagerness to create a minature world,mionveri is the physical manifestation of an extremely creative mind. The journey began in a cramped Houston apartment, transforming into, everyday cardboard into architectual peices. In time, it grew from cardboard into wearable art, first through timepeices sculpted from scratch, and later through a needle, thread, and sewing machine. Translating the weight of human experience into physical objects.
